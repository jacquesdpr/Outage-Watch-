About this board

This board pulls from OurPower.co.za, an independent tracker that reads the City's public alerts feed every ~15 min. It refreshes itself automatically every 30 minutes; tap "Check power / water" on any card to jump to the live page for full detail.

Burgundy Estate and Sitari are supplied directly by Eskom rather than the City of Cape Town — marked "Eskom" under the ELEC tag on those cards — so their power status reflects Eskom's own outage reporting, not the City's feed.
